# Sales and pilot kit — draft

## Positioning
A configurable operations workspace for small organisations that currently manage volunteers, people, tasks, equipment and events across spreadsheets, paper forms and separate apps.

## Example pitch
**Spend less time chasing paperwork and more time running your organisation.**

Our configurable operations dashboard brings the information your team needs into one place. Choose the modules that fit your organisation — from people and attendance to events, maintenance and reporting — without buying a complicated system you do not need.

**Current availability:** prototype demonstrations only. Real customer accounts, data storage and commercial service are not yet available.

## Candidate customers
- Heritage railways and museums: volunteer coordination, maintenance, event planning and reporting
- Community organisations and charities: people, shifts, sessions, tasks and room use
- Small maintenance businesses: work orders, assets, inspections and staff schedules
- Visitor attractions: staffing, opening checks, equipment and events

## Discovery interview (15–20 minutes)
1. What are the three most time-consuming administrative tasks each week?
2. Which spreadsheets, paper records or apps do you currently use?
3. How many people would use the system, and on which devices?
4. What information must be restricted to specific roles?
5. Which reports or printable records do you need?
6. What would make switching worthwhile? What would stop you?
7. What level of monthly cost could you realistically approve?
8. Would you be interested in reviewing a fictional-data prototype?

Do not ask prospects to upload real records during discovery.

## Suggested demonstration flow
1. Show the sector-specific overview and navigation.
2. Explain configurable modules using the owner demo.
3. Walk through a sample task, attendance or maintenance workflow.
4. Show what is **not yet implemented**, including login, persistence and billing.
5. Collect feedback and ask permission to follow up when a secure pilot is ready.

## Email draft — exploratory, not ready-to-sell claim
Subject: Could I get your feedback on an operations dashboard concept?

Hello,

I'm developing a configurable operations dashboard aimed at smaller organisations that need a simpler way to manage everyday administration.

The concept brings together features such as volunteer or staff coordination, attendance, maintenance tasks, events and reporting, with the modules tailored to each organisation.

I'm currently looking for feedback on a prototype, rather than selling a finished service. Would someone on your team be willing to spend 15–20 minutes looking at a demonstration using fictional information and telling me what would or would not be useful?

Thank you for considering it.

Kind regards,
[Your name]

## Pilot proposal outline
- Customer objectives and success measures
- Scope: modules and user roles
- Timeline and support arrangements
- Data protection responsibilities and permitted data
- Security and backup arrangements
- Exit plan and export of customer data
- Price or free-trial terms, clearly agreed in writing
- Acceptance and review checkpoints

## Pricing research — no unverified promises
Do not advertise fixed prices until hosting, support, payment fees, tax, development and security costs are estimated. Test whether customers prefer a base monthly fee with optional modules or tiers based on organisation size. Avoid charging for unfinished functionality.

## Marketing claims to avoid
Do not say 'fully secure', 'GDPR compliant', 'used by EVLT', 'trusted by customers', 'live 24/7' or 'ready to deploy' without evidence and permission. The EVLT dashboard is a separate project; no endorsement is implied.
