# Sales and operations starter pack

## Lead qualification
Organisation; sector; number of staff/volunteers; sites; current tools; top pain; estimated admin time; budget owner; buying timeline; privacy needs; desired pilot modules; next action.

## Discovery call
Ask for a walkthrough of the current process. Identify one painful repeated workflow and what a successful improvement looks like. Do not promise custom work before assessing support cost.

## Pilot agreement outline (legal review required)
Scope and named modules; start/end dates; test users; permitted data; controller/processor responsibilities; confidentiality; incident contacts; support hours; backups; export/deletion; fees if any; termination; no guarantee of production readiness.

## Onboarding checklist
Confirm contract/privacy arrangements; nominate company admin; configure tenant and sites; assign roles; migrate minimum required records; run cross-tenant access tests; train admin; verify exports; establish support contact.

## Support playbook
Severity 1: customer data exposure or total outage — stop unsafe operations, assess incident, notify appropriate parties according to legal requirements.
Severity 2: core workflow blocked — workaround and prioritise.
Severity 3: cosmetic/low-impact defect — backlog.
Log reproducible steps, affected organisation, time, impact and resolution without copying sensitive data into tickets.

## Pricing worksheet
Monthly revenue = paying organisations × average monthly fee.
Monthly contribution = revenue − hosting − payment processing − variable support costs.
Example only: 10 organisations × £35 = £350 monthly gross revenue before costs and tax. This is a scenario, not a forecast.
Track onboarding hours, support hours, churn and failed payments.

## Launch criteria
Do not sell or onboard real customer data until authentication, tenant isolation, backups, privacy paperwork, incident handling, security tests and support capacity are demonstrated.
