# Owner Admin — Business plan (working draft, 6 November 2026)

## Positioning
A simple, modular operations platform for UK volunteer-led heritage attractions, small charities, community organisations and small operational teams. EVLT is a development reference, **not** a connected customer in Owner Admin.

## Customer problem
Fragmented spreadsheets, paper attendance logs, maintenance records and volunteer rotas create duplicate admin and make oversight harder. The 2026 Charity Digital Skills Report says 78% of surveyed small charities were at an early stage digitally, 63% cited squeezed finances and 47% prioritised better data collection/analysis. This is directional survey evidence, not a count of potential customers.
Source: https://charitydigitalskills.co.uk/report/detailed-findings/small-charities/

## Target segments and first offer
1. Heritage railways, museums and volunteer-run visitor attractions — strongest fit with EVLT experience.
2. Community centres and local charities — volunteer rota, hours, events, reports.
3. Small maintenance or facilities teams — work orders, asset register, inspections.
Do not try to serve every sector immediately. Sell the outcome (less admin and reliable records), not a long feature list.

## Proposed product packages (HYPOTHESES, not validated prices)
- Pilot: free/low-cost limited trial, fixed duration, no production data until security is verified.
- Starter: £15–£30 per organisation/month; volunteers, attendance, basic reporting.
- Operations: £40–£75 per organisation/month; maintenance, events, exports, permissions.
- Custom: quoted after requirements review; onboarding and support priced separately.
Validate willingness to pay with interviews before publishing prices. Do not promise service levels or security certifications not yet achieved.

## Acquisition plan
Interview 8–12 organisations, starting with heritage and volunteer-led operations. Ask what they use now, hours spent on admin, recurring mistakes, decision maker, annual software budget, procurement and privacy concerns. Show a prototype only after explaining it is a demo. Seek 2–3 pilot commitments; don't treat verbal enthusiasm as a sale.

## Basic unit economics to track
Monthly recurring revenue = sum of paying organisation subscriptions.
Gross margin = (revenue - hosting - payment fees - variable support) / revenue.
Activation = organisations completing setup and recording first useful workflow.
Retention = paid organisations still subscribed after 3/6/12 months.
Track time spent onboarding/supporting each customer; a cheap plan can lose money if setup is bespoke.

## Decisions pending
Business name/brand, legal structure, actual pricing, hosting, data region, support commitments, payment processor, contracts, retention periods and insurance. Get appropriate UK legal/accounting advice before trading.

## Current reality
Owner Admin is a public, unauthenticated GitHub Pages demo using ephemeral fictional data. It is **not** a live multi-tenant service and must not accept real personal or customer data.
