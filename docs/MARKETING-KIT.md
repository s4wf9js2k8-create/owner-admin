# Marketing kit — draft, not for publication until product is secure

## Working proposition
Simple operations software for volunteer-led organisations, heritage attractions and small teams. Manage people, attendance, maintenance and events from one place, with only the modules you need.

## Website hero
Headline: Less paperwork. More time for what matters.
Subheading: A straightforward operations platform for organisations that run on people, places and practical work.
Primary action: Request an early-access conversation
Secondary action: Explore example features
Trust statement: Currently in development. No production customer accounts available.

## Benefits
- Keep volunteer and staff records organised.
- See attendance, maintenance and events in one workspace.
- Choose relevant modules without unnecessary complexity.
- Give administrators role-appropriate access (planned; not yet implemented).
- Export useful records for reporting (planned).

## Example social post
We're exploring a simpler way for heritage attractions, charities and small operational teams to manage their everyday administration. Our prototype brings together volunteer coordination, attendance, maintenance and events. We're speaking with organisations to learn what would make their work easier. Interested in giving feedback? Get in touch. This is an early prototype, not a live service.

## Short introductory email
Subject: Could we ask about your organisation's admin processes?
Hello,
I'm developing a modular operations system for small organisations, initially focusing on volunteer-led heritage and community sites. I'm researching how teams manage attendance, events, maintenance and records today.
Would someone involved in your day-to-day operations be willing to spare 20 minutes to discuss what works and what causes difficulties? This is research, not a sales pitch, and the software is not yet ready for production use.
Thank you.

## Campaign experiments
1. Ten research conversations with heritage or community organisations.
2. Two alternative value propositions: volunteer time-saving versus maintenance oversight.
3. One-page landing page with early-access interest form only after privacy notice and safe form storage.
4. Measure conversations booked, pain points repeated, pilot interest, and eventual willingness to pay.
Do not use EVLT's name or logo as an endorsement without permission.
