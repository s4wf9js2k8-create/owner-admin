## Architecture

Specialised components remain separate under `src/completion/`, with pure metrics and business validation outside the views. The fictional adapter validates related changes before one state publication for supported multi-record commands. Record history and schemas are retained for archive/form workflows. The old state-only forms component is replaced by the record-based workspace.

Specialist pages continue to load on demand. `qrcode-generator` is the only added dependency and creates labels locally. No EVLT resources, Vercel, paid infrastructure, external messages or real customer systems are used. The Node/PostgreSQL/Docker preparation is preserved.
