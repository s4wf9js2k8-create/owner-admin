# Customer interview and pilot kit

## Interview questions (20–30 minutes)
1. What are your main operational tasks each week?
2. What paper forms, spreadsheets or tools do you use today?
3. Which tasks consume the most volunteer/staff time?
4. Where do records go missing or become inconsistent?
5. Who needs to access which records? Any sensitive information?
6. How many sites, administrators, volunteers and active users?
7. Which features would you use weekly: attendance, rota, maintenance, events, reporting?
8. What would make you stop using the product?
9. Who approves spending and what budget range is realistic?
10. Would you test a secure pilot when ready? What success criteria matter?

## Pilot success scorecard
- Time to first useful action (minutes).
- Weekly active organisations (not merely signups).
- Admin hours saved (self-reported baseline vs pilot).
- Data quality / missing records.
- Support tickets per organisation.
- Willingness to pay after pilot.
- Customer satisfaction and reasons for rejection.

## Demo script
Explain the public prototype contains fictional data and no real logins. Walk through Organisations → Company Profile → Modules → Support. Ask customer to describe how they would perform their actual workflow. Record confusing steps and missing functions. Never enter personal details into the prototype.

## Simple outreach draft
Subject: Researching a simpler operations system for volunteer-led organisations

Hello,

I'm developing a modular operations platform aimed at helping smaller organisations manage volunteers, attendance, maintenance and events without juggling multiple spreadsheets.

I'm currently speaking to organisations to understand their workflows, rather than selling a finished product. Would someone involved in day-to-day administration be willing to spare 20 minutes to share what works well and what causes difficulties?

Thanks for considering it.
